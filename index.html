Oyetunji E. Ogundijo, PhD

Electrical Engineering • Statistical Signal Processing • Computational Biology

Academic Background

Oyetunji E. Ogundijo is a computational scientist specializing in statistical signal processing, Bayesian inference, sequential Monte Carlo methods, and cancer genomics. He earned his PhD in Electrical Engineering from Columbia University, conducting doctoral research with Prof. Xiaodong Wang and Prof. Dimitris Anastassiou.

Education

  • Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering — Columbia University, New York, NY
  • B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering — Obafemi Awolowo University, Ile-Ife, Nigeria

Research Areas

  • Computational Genomics & Intra-Tumor Heterogeneity
  • Gene Expression Deconvolution & Statistical Signal Processing
